People

We work with a group of strong, self-motivated, and talented students on safe AI and robot learning.

Principal Investigator

Prof. Shangding Gu (顾尚定)

Principal Investigator of SAIL Lab · Tenure-track Associate Professor, School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Shangding Gu received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Technical University of Munich (advised by Prof. Alois Knoll) and was a postdoctoral researcher at UC Berkeley (working with Prof. Costas Spanos and Prof. Dawn Song) before joining SJTU. His research spans safe reinforcement learning, robot learning, LLM agents, and trustworthy AI. See his Google Scholar profile for the full publication record.
