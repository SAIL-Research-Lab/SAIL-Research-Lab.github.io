Join SAIL Lab

We are always looking for talented and motivated people to join us.

SAIL Lab works on AI safety, machine learning, large language models (and agents), and robotics — with the goal of building learning systems that are safe, reliable, and efficient enough to be deployed in the real world. We are looking for highly self-motivated students who are passionate about these topics and enjoy doing research that matters.

Openings

  • Ph.D. / M.S. students — via the normal admission of the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

  • Undergraduate students — interested in doing research in the lab (e.g., as a research assistant or for a thesis project).

  • Visiting students / research interns — we welcome both international and domestic visiting students and interns.

What We Look For

  • Strong self-motivation, curiosity, and perseverance in tackling hard research problems.

  • Solid background in machine learning, reinforcement learning, optimization, and/or robotics; solid programming skills (e.g., Python and deep-learning frameworks).

  • Good communication and writing skills (the lab works in an international environment).

How to Apply

If you are interested in our research, please email our lab recruiting email at sail.lab.hr@gmail.com with details about your background and relevant skills.

  • Use a clear subject line, e.g., “[Prospective PhD Student/Master Student/Intern] Your name – your university/organization”.

  • Introduce yourself briefly and describe your research interests and experience. Attaching a CV (and transcripts, if available) is very helpful.

  • Mention any relevant work — for example, code repositories, projects, or papers you have written.

To get a feeling for the lab’s research topics, please browse our publications, projects, and open-source softwares, or watch our tutorials and talks.
