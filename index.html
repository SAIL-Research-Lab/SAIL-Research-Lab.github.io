Welcome to SAIL Lab

Safe AI and Robot Learning Lab — School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

We are a research group working on reinforcement learning, planning, and AI safety, with applications in foundation models (e.g., large language models and multimodal models), robotics, and autonomous systems. Our goal is to design safe, reliable, and efficient learning systems that address pressing real-world challenges — from trustworthy decision-making for embodied agents to scalable and dependable AI. The lab is led by Prof. Shangding Gu (顾尚定).

We are looking for highly self-motivated undergraduate students, graduate (MS/PhD) students, and visiting students who are passionate about AI safety, machine learning, large language models, and robotics. If you are interested in our research, please contact us with details about your background and relevant skills.

Research

Concretely, the lab builds algorithms, theory, benchmarks, and open-source systems for safe decision-making under uncertainty — spanning safe reinforcement learning, robot learning, and LLM-based agents — and evaluates them in robotics, autonomous driving, smart grid, and semiconductor-manufacturing scenarios.
